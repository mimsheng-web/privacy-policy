這個應用程式是什麼

auto_reels_01 是經營者自己使用的內部工具，用途只有一個：把經營者自己製作的影片，發佈到經營者自己管理的 Instagram 帳號與 Facebook 粉絲專頁，並讀取這些貼文的成效數字。它不對外開放註冊，也不提供給一般大眾登入使用。

我們會接觸到哪些資料

  • 經營者自己帳號的存取權杖：用來代表這些帳號發佈內容與讀取成效，只存放在經營者自己的電腦上。
  • 經營者自己貼文的成效數字：例如觀看次數、按讚數、留言數、分享數。
  • 經營者自己貼文底下的公開留言：留言者公開顯示的帳號名稱與留言內容，只用來查看與回覆留言。

除此之外，我們不蒐集其他使用者的個人資料，不追蹤任何人，也不使用 Cookie。

資料怎麼使用、會不會給別人

上面這些資料只用於發佈內容、回覆留言與檢視成效。我們不出售、不出租、不提供給任何第三方，也不用於廣告投放。

資料保存多久

成效數字與留言紀錄保存在經營者自己的電腦上，供內部檢視使用。存取權杖在失效或撤銷後即不再使用。

如何要求刪除資料

如果你曾在我們的貼文底下留言，希望我們刪除手邊關於你的留言紀錄，請透過發佈該貼文的 Instagram 帳號或 Facebook 粉絲專頁私訊我們，我們會在合理時間內處理。你也可以隨時自行刪除自己的留言。

政策變更

這份政策如有更新，會直接修改本頁並更新上方的日期。

Privacy Policy

For the app auto_reels_01 · Last updated: October 9, 2026

What this app is

auto_reels_01 is an internal tool used only by its operator. Its sole purpose is to publish the operator's own videos to Instagram accounts and Facebook Pages that the operator manages, and to read performance figures for those posts. It is not open to the public and offers no sign-up or login for other people.

Data we handle

  • Access tokens for the operator's own accounts, used to publish content and read performance on their behalf. They are stored only on the operator's own computer.
  • Performance figures for the operator's own posts, such as views, likes, comments and shares.
  • Public comments on the operator's own posts: the commenter's publicly shown account name and the comment text, used only to read and reply to comments.

Beyond this, we do not collect personal data about other people, we do not track anyone, and we do not use cookies.

How data is used and shared

The data above is used only to publish content, reply to comments and review performance. We do not sell, rent or share it with any third party, and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention

Performance figures and comment records are kept on the operator's own computer for internal review. Access tokens are no longer used once they expire or are revoked.

Requesting deletion

If you have commented on one of our posts and would like us to delete our record of your comment, please message the Instagram account or Facebook Page that published the post. We will handle the request within a reasonable time. You can also delete your own comment at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the date above.
